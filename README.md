# Stochastic-Lab

### From Randomness to Optimal Decisions

**Stochastic Processes | Stochastic Calculus | Dynamic Programming | Stochastic Control**

## Overview

**Stochastic-Lab** is a personal computational mathematics repository focused on exploring stochastic systems through mathematical theory, numerical methods, and Python simulations.

The repository aims to bridge the gap between **mathematical concepts and computational implementation**, developing a deeper understanding of how randomness evolves over time and how optimal decisions can be made under uncertainty.

It covers fundamental topics in stochastic processes, stochastic calculus, dynamic programming, and stochastic optimal control, with applications in quantitative finance and mathematical modeling.

## Objectives

- Explore the mathematical foundations of stochastic processes and their properties.
- Implement stochastic models and numerical simulations using Python.
- Understand stochastic differential equations and their numerical solutions.
- Investigate dynamic programming and optimal decision-making under uncertainty.
- Apply stochastic modeling and optimization techniques to problems in quantitative finance.

## Areas of Exploration

**Stochastic Processes**

Study random phenomena evolving over time, including Brownian motion, Markov chains, Poisson processes, and martingales.

**Stochastic Calculus**

Explore Itô calculus, stochastic differential equations, and numerical methods for modeling continuous-time random systems.

**Dynamic Programming**

Investigate Bellman's principle of optimality, value functions, and recursive optimization methods.

**Stochastic Control**

Study optimal decision-making in stochastic environments using controlled differential equations and Hamilton–Jacobi–Bellman (HJB) equations.

**Computational Finance**

Explore applications in derivative pricing, portfolio optimization, interest-rate modeling, and financial risk management.

## Tools

Python · NumPy · SciPy · Matplotlib · Pandas · Jupyter Notebook
